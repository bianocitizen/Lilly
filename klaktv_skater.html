About
Marc Johnson
Street skating redefined by precision
Born in 1975 in Reno, Nevada, Marc Johnson turned professional in the mid-90s and spent three decades redefining what street skating could look and feel like — quiet power, exact technique, no wasted motion.
Early Career
From Reno to Girl Skateboards
MJ's early footage circulated on VHS tapes before Girl Skateboards signed him. His part in Mouse (1996) introduced a generation to a skater who seemed incapable of an awkward move.
Legacy
Co-founder · Lakai & Fourstar
Beyond skating, MJ co-founded Lakai Footwear in 1999 with Guy Mariano and others, creating one of the most influential skate shoe companies of the 2000s. Fourstar Clothing followed in 2003.
